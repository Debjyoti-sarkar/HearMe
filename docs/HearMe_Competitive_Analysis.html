1. Executive Summary

HearMe is a React Native / Expo based women's personal safety app targeting the Indian market. It already ships a set of features that are common across mainstream safety apps (shake-to-SOS, journey monitor, evidence locker, fake call, emergency SMS) and one category of features that is genuinely novel: behavioural biometrics — panic-pattern tap detection, coercion hesitation, phone-handoff detection, and a 0–100 Trust Engine score.

Core insight: HearMe's behavioural biometrics are not present in any mainstream women's safety app on the Play Store. This is the moat. The opportunity is to (a) close the parity gap on standard SOS features and (b) stack coercion-resistant UX patterns on top of the biometric core — creating an app attackers cannot easily disable even with the phone in hand.

4. Unique Differentiators to Add (Your Moat)

These features, to the best of current market knowledge, are not shipped by any mainstream competitor in the personal-safety category.

Unique
1. Duress PIN / Reverse PIN Unlock
Correct PIN unlocks normally; reverse PIN unlocks a decoy home screen while silently triggering SOS. A banking-grade pattern absent from every mainstream safety app.
Unique
2. Disguised-UI Mode
Launcher icon and splash can impersonate a calculator, notes, or weather app. Single toggle in settings. Lifesaving in forced-unlock or phone-snatch scenarios.
Unique
3. Voice-Keyword Trigger
On-device Whisper / Vosk listens for a user-defined safe word (e.g. "mausam kaisa hai"). Zero cloud upload until triggered. Works screen-off.
Unique
4. Coercion-Aware Fake Deactivation
If an attacker forces the user to uninstall or disable, the app appears to turn off but retains a 30-minute grace trigger. Requires Android foreground-service hygiene.
Unique
5. AI Threat-Narrator
During an active SOS, an on-device LLM (Gemini Nano) auto-transcribes recorded audio and generates a timeline summary — e.g. "raised voices at 21:04, vehicle start at 21:06" — attached to the evidence bundle.
Unique
6. Bluetooth-Mesh SOS
When no cellular is available, broadcast SOS to any HearMe user within ~100 m (Bridgefy-style mesh). Critical for metros, tunnels, rural zones.
Unique
7. Safe-Route AI
Overlay on Google Maps that re-routes around low-safety zones using your crowd heatmap and NCRB open-crime data (India).
Unique
8. Guardian Mode (Peer Live-Watch)
Any trusted contact across the city can live-watch your journey on their phone and confirm "I'm watching." Reduces escalation burden on police.
Unique
9. Post-Incident Auto-FIR Draft
Uses evidence timeline + location + transcript to pre-fill an FIR PDF compatible with state police portals. Major value in India.
Unique
10. Wearable & Panic-Button Integration
Apple Watch, Noise / boAt smartwatches, and Bluetooth keychain panic buttons (~₹300). Triple-press triggers SOS even when the phone is locked in a bag.

6. Trust, Compliance & Monetisation

  • Subscription tier (Noonlight model): 24/7 human dispatcher via a partnered private-security firm.
  • Compliance: SOC 2 + India DPDP Act badges in onboarding — trust is a conversion lever.
  • Open-source the Trust Engine: reinforces credibility (you already adapted PhishSafe) and invites academic validation.
  • Institutional partnerships: Ola / Uber / Rapido integration; campus safety programmes; NGO distribution.

  • AI Threat-Narrator

  • Safe-Route AI overlay
  • Family Circle / group journeys

Recommendation: Start with Duress PIN, Disguised UI, and Voice Trigger. Combined with the existing behavioural biometrics, these three features alone put HearMe ahead of every mainstream safety app on the Play Store today — and each is achievable in a single sprint using the stack already in place.

8. Risks & Mitigations

RiskMitigation
False positives on behavioural alertsGraduated response: local vibration → check-in prompt → SOS after 15 s no-cancel.
Battery drain from always-on sensors / voice triggerKeep voice-keyword model on-device, VAD-gated; sensors sampled at 5 Hz off-motion.
Evidence tampering allegations in courtHash-chain + Supabase Object Lock + server-side timestamp signature.
Police-API reliability across statesFallback: SMS to 112 + WhatsApp Business template + email to state helpline.
Disguised-UI rejection on iOSUse Alternate Icons API (iOS-approved) rather than full launcher spoof.
